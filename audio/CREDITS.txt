Wind: SketchMan3, wind whoosh loop (2019)
https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop
Based on JaggedStone, Loopable Dungeon Ambience (2014), also CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience

Edits: individual gunshots isolated; silence trimmed; one-shot peaks normalized;
foley downsampled to mono 22050Hz; wind mixed into ambience; a two-second
endpoint overlap added for ambience looping; MP3 compression. In-game one-shots
have small playback-rate variations. These are edited samples, not endorsements
by their original creators. Liquid, flame, tire, breath, heartbeat, horn and
engine textures are generated by the game's audio engine, not these packs.

Storm and campfire audio (user-selected additions):

Thunder: cognito perceptu, Thunder crack 2, CC0 1.0.
https://uncommonear.com/t/fd-254427693e/thunder-crack-2-by-cognito-perceptu
-> thunder.mp3. Real recorded thunder crack; plays on storm lightning 0.35s
after the flash, at most once per 8.5s, quieter when sheltered. Replaces the
previous synthesized rumble. Edited: two-pass loudness normalization to
-22 LUFS/-2 dBTP, mono 32kHz/96kbps MP3. Small playback-rate variation at
runtime. No creator endorsement implied.

Campfire loop: Sufyan_Khalil, Fire Place, Pixabay Content License.
https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/fire-place-189399/
License: https://pixabay.com/service/terms/
A 30-second seamless loop edited from the recording (section trimmed,
endpoint crossfade, loudness-normalized) -> fireplace.mp3, bundled only as an
incorporated game asset; the unmodified recording is not redistributed.
Plays as a positional HRTF loop that swells as the player approaches a lit
campfire and fades with distance. No creator endorsement implied.

Cold breathing: deleted_user_2104797, sound 166125, CC0 1.0.
https://freesound.org/people/deleted_user_2104797/sounds/166125/
-> cold.mp3. Shaky scared breathing; loops while the player is chilled or
freezing, fading in and out with the thermal state. Edited per the user's
audition approvals: 15% faster tempo with pitch preserved, level reduced to
the approved quieter version, short endpoint fades, mono 32kHz/96kbps MP3.
No creator endorsement implied.

Teeth chattering: mickdow, "ShiveringTeeth", CC0 1.0.
