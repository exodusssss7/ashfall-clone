ASHFALL audio credits

These sound assets are offered under Creative Commons CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Credits are retained to document their creators and sources.

Footsteps and impacts: Kenney, Impact Sounds
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Pack mirror: https://cc0-sounds.exi.software/collection/kenney_impactsounds/

https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio

Gunshots: The Free Firearm Sound Library (2014)
Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
1911 A_34P and Mossberg N_26P, edited into individual shots.

https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
First Shell and Rack.

https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling
GhostMoan01 through GhostMoan04.

Day ambience: TinyWorlds, Forest Ambience (2014)
https://opengameart.org/content/forest-ambience

Night ambience: congusbongus, Lost in a bad place (2022)
https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop

Wind: SketchMan3, wind whoosh loop (2019)
https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop
Based on JaggedStone, Loopable Dungeon Ambience (2014), also CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience

Edits: individual gunshots isolated; silence trimmed; one-shot peaks normalized;
foley downsampled to mono 22050Hz; wind mixed into ambience; a two-second
endpoint overlap added for ambience looping; MP3 compression. In-game one-shots
have small playback-rate variations. These are edited samples, not endorsements
by their original creators. Liquid, flame, tire, breath, heartbeat, horn and
engine textures are generated by the game's audio engine, not these packs.

New action samples (current improvement pass, all CC0 1.0):

Crowbar air swings: artisticdude, Swishes Sound Pack (2011)
https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
swish-7.wav, swish-8.wav, swish-9.wav -> swing0/1/2.mp3.

https://opengameart.org/content/pain-sounds-by-emopreben
Tracks 2 Hurgh, 5 Urggh, 1 Arrrch -> hurt0/1/2.mp3.

Bottle/drinking/refuel handling: qubodup, Liquid Bottle Drink Set (2011)
https://opengameart.org/content/liquid-bottle-drink-set
swallow-03, bottle-open-02, bottle-shake-03 -> drink, bottleOpen, liquid.mp3.
The bottle-shake recording is used as a short refuelling liquid accent.

Repair tools: Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero), Mechanical Sounds (2020)
https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds
mechanical2.wav, rattle1.wav -> repair0/1.mp3.

Fishing splash: jcpmcdonald, Skippy Fish Water Sound Collection (2014)
https://opengameart.org/content/skippy-fish-water-sound-collection
waterReentry.wav -> splash.mp3.

Edits to the above: high-pass 80Hz, low-pass 10kHz, leading silence trim,
short endpoint fades, mono 32kHz MP3 at 96kbps, peak normalized to -3dBFS.
Runtime gain is lower, with small pitch variation and a shared compressor.
No creator endorsement is implied. Source pages list these works as CC0.

Surface-specific footsteps:
- Kenney, Impact Sounds (CC0), footstep_wood_000..003 -> planks0..3;
  footstep_carpet_000..003 -> carpet0..3.
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  Download mirror: https://cc0-sounds.exi.software/collection/kenney_impactsounds/
- Fantozzi, sliced/submitted by qubodup, Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone), CC0.
  SandL1/R1/L2/R2 -> dirt0..3 (loose-earth/grit interpretation).
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
These 12 samples: high-pass 70Hz, low-pass 11kHz, leading-silence trim,
short endpoint fades, mono 32kHz/96kbps MP3, -3dBFS peak.
Original concrete and grass step banks remain. Planks cover wood floors,
porches and pier; carpet covers rugs; tile covers bath/clinic/diner floors.
No walking/wading is added for impassable water. No creator endorsement implied.

Tile footsteps, user-selected option B:
sgcardinal, Footsteps - Soft Soled Shoes on Tile Floor (2017), CC0.
https://freesound.org/people/sgcardinal/sounds/395075/
Four individual steps cut from the openly served high-quality MP3 preview
of soft-soled shoes on a bathroom tile floor -> tile0..3.mp3.
High-pass 90Hz, low-pass 8.5kHz, trimmed/faded, mono 32kHz/96kbps,
-3dBFS sample peak. Runtime gain 0.16 (running 1.28x). No endorsement implied.
Replaces the rejected Fantozzi Stone tile interpretation; those stone samples
are not bundled. The unselected Nox_Sound option A is not bundled either.

v1.0 additions (all CC0 1.0):

LMG fire: Tabasco, Gunshot Sounds (2010)
https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
SKS rifle shots cut from sks.wav (shots at 0.3s and 7.7s) -> lmg0/1.mp3.
Real recorded semi-auto rifle crack; high-pass 75Hz, low-pass 10kHz, trimmed,
short fades, mono 32kHz/96kbps MP3, -3dBFS peak. Runtime gain 0.5 with small
pitch variation; full-auto cadence comes from the game's fire rate.

Zombie kill and car-vs-zombie impacts: Independent.nu (via qubodup),
8 wet squish, slurp impacts (2009)
https://opengameart.org/content/8-wet-squish-slurp-impacts
impactsplat03 -> zombiedie0, impactsplat07 -> zombiedie1,
impactsplat01 -> carhit0, impactsplat04 -> carhit1.
High-pass 60-70Hz, low-pass 10kHz, trimmed, endpoint fades, mono 32kHz/96kbps,
-3dBFS peak. The car impact layers a quiet existing metal hit on top.

Zombie hurt reactions: rubberduck, 80 CC0 creature SFX
https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
hurt_02, hurt_04, hurt_05 -> zhurt0/1/2.mp3.
High-pass 90Hz, low-pass 9.5kHz, endpoint fades, mono 32kHz/96kbps, -3dBFS
peak. Per-archetype playback rate (bloater lowest, runner highest), throttled
to 6/s so automatic fire cannot spam it. No creator endorsement implied.
